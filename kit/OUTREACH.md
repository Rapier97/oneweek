# Outreach messages

Ten short messages for people Alex already knows or has just met. Text, WhatsApp or LinkedIn length. Swap the angle brackets, keep it casual, send one at a time.

Proof line, use as is: "just built a rota app for an activity company in a week".

---

**1. Someone you just met**
Good to meet you at <place> today. I build apps and websites for small businesses, one a week, £2k fixed. Just built a rota app for an activity company in a week. Is there anything in your business you are still doing on paper or in a spreadsheet?

**2. An old contact**
<Name>, long time. I've gone out on my own: apps, websites and AI for small businesses, built in a week for a fixed £2k. Just did a rota app for an activity company. Who do you know who's running something off a spreadsheet that should be an app?

**3. A local business owner you already use**
Hi <Name>, it's Alex, <the customer who ...>. I build software for small businesses now, one thing a week for a fixed £2k. Just built a rota app for an activity company in a week. Is there anything at <business> that is a pain every week that I could take a look at?

**4. A referral from a previous client**
Hi <Name>, <Client> said I should get in touch. I built their <thing> in a week, fixed price, and they mentioned you might have something similar going on. Have you got 15 minutes this week for a quick call?

**5. Follow-up when there's no reply**
Hi <Name>, nudging this up in case it got buried. No worries if the timing's wrong. Is it worth a quick chat, or shall I leave it for now?

**6. Someone who said "maybe later"**
Hi <Name>, you said to check back around <month>. Still building one thing a week for small businesses, £2k fixed, and I've got a week free on <date>. Is now a better time?

**7. Someone with a bad or missing website**
Hi <Name>, I was looking for <business> online and <couldn't find a site / the site doesn't work on a phone>. I build websites for small businesses in a week for a fixed £2k, just finished a rota app for an activity company. Want me to send a quick sketch of what yours could look like?

**8. Someone who complained about admin**
Hi <Name>, you mentioned <the thing they complained about> the other day. That is exactly the sort of thing I build: one app, one week, £2k fixed. Just built a rota app for an activity company that way. Want me to show you how it could work for you?

**9. A friend or family member (ask for intros, not work)**
Hi <Name>, quick favour. I'm building apps and websites for small businesses, one a week for a fixed £2k, and I'm after my next few clients. Just built a rota app for an activity company in a week. Can you think of two people who run a business and are drowning in admin?

**10. A previous client, a month on**
Hi <Name>, how's the <thing> holding up? If anything's bugging you, send it over. Also, I'm booking weeks for <month>. Is there anyone you'd point my way?
