# Done in a Week kit

Used in this order: first call, then QUOTE, then SPEC, then build, then HANDOVER, then OUTREACH.

- `QUOTE.md`: the email sent straight after the first call. Confirms the one thing, the week, £2,000, and what the client needs to send.
- `SPEC_TEMPLATE.md`: the one-page spec filled in on day 1 with the client. Copy it into the client's folder and keep the Decisions log current.
- `HANDOVER.md`: the last-day checklist, plus the case study paragraph template.
- `OUTREACH.md`: ten short messages for finding the next client, including the two introductions asked for at handover.
