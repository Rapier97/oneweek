# Handover checklist

Last day of the week. Do these in order, on a call with the client.

## Before the call
- [ ] Everything in the spec's Screens and Rules works, tested as each role
- [ ] Spec updated: Decisions log and Not in this week are current
- [ ] Balance invoice ready to send

## On the call
- [ ] Client walks through it live, driving, on their own device. Fix anything small there and then; note anything bigger for a second week
- [ ] Hand over accounts and logins: hosting, domain, database, app store, email sending, anything else. Owner is the client's email, not Alex's
- [ ] Say it plainly: once the balance is paid, the code and accounts are theirs. Show them where the code lives
- [ ] Offer the £200 a month plan: hosting, fixes and small changes. Yes, no or think about it are all fine
- [ ] Ask for one sentence they would be happy to have quoted
- [ ] Ask for two introductions: "Who else do you know who is running their business off a spreadsheet or a notebook?"
- [ ] Send the balance invoice

## After the call
- [ ] Write the case study paragraph (below) and add it to the site
- [ ] Message the two introductions within 24 hours (see OUTREACH.md)
- [ ] Diary: check in with the client in two weeks

## Case study paragraph

One paragraph, no more. Before, after, one week.

> **<Client type>, <town>.** Before: <what they were doing and what was going wrong, one sentence>. After: <what they have now and what it changed, one or two sentences, with a number if there is one>. Built and handed over in one week, <Mon YYYY>. "<Their one sentence.>"

Example:

> **Activity company, Portsmouth.** Before: the owner ran a 20-person rota from a spreadsheet, chasing availability by text and working out expenses by hand. After: staff mark their free days and log their miles in a phone app, the owner builds the week in minutes and sees exactly who is owed what. Built and handed over in one week, November 2026. "<Quote>."
