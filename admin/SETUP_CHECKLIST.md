# Setup checklist

Everything to do once to trade legally and simply as a UK sole trader. Figures checked online on 28 Oct 2026 and are mostly from accountancy and comparison sites rather than GOV.UK, so confirm each on the linked page before acting. Not legal or tax advice.

Order to do them: 1, 2, 3, 8, 4, then the rest when there is a quiet hour.

## 1. Tell HMRC you are self-employed

- **What:** Register for Self Assessment as a sole trader. You get a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR).
- **Why:** Required once you earn over £1,000 a year from self-employment. Pegasus was the first paid work in Oct 2026, which is the 2026/27 tax year.
- **Deadline:** 5 October 2027 (the 5 October after the end of the tax year you started in). Do it now anyway; it is quick and late registration can carry a penalty.
- **Then:** First tax return for 2026/27 due online by 31 January 2028, with the tax. From the second year HMRC asks for payments on account in January and July, so year two feels like paying 1.5 years of tax. Keep that in mind when setting money aside.
- **Time:** 20 minutes online, UTR arrives in about 10 working days.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Link:** https://www.gov.uk/register-for-self-assessment
- **Sources:** [Bytestart on the 5 October deadline](https://www.bytestart.co.uk/news-insights/october-5th-self-assessment-deadline/), [Zoho guide to registering](https://www.zoho.com/uk/books/academy/taxes-and-compliance/register-as-self-employed-uk.html)

## 2. Separate bank account

- **What:** A business current account that only Oneweek money goes through.
- **Why:** Not legally required for a sole trader, but it makes bookkeeping and the tax return a 10 minute job instead of a weekend. Clients also expect to pay "Oneweek" not a personal name.
- **Options, all free with no monthly fee:**
  - **Starling Business:** free, good app, free Post Office cash deposits up to £1,000 a month. Simplest pick.
  - **Mettle (NatWest):** free, includes FreeAgent bookkeeping free, which covers item 3. No cash deposits. Best value if you want bookkeeping thrown in.
  - **Monzo Business Lite:** free; Pro at £9 a month adds tax pots and invoicing. Tide also has a free tier but charges 20p a transfer after 5 a month.
- **Time:** 15 minutes to apply, usually open within a day or two.
- **Cost:** £0.
- **Sources:** [Finder free business accounts 2026](https://www.finder.com/uk/compare-free-business-bank-accounts), [SmallBusiness.co.uk sole trader accounts](https://smallbusiness.co.uk/best-business-bank-accounts-for-sole-traders-2563034/)

## 3. Simple bookkeeping

- **What:** One place that records every invoice in and every expense out. Either FreeAgent (free with Mettle) or a spreadsheet with two tabs: income, expenses. Keep receipts as photos in one folder.
- **Why:** You need it for the tax return, and from April 2027 for Making Tax Digital (see item 5).
- **How:** Friday 4pm slot: log the week's invoices and any expenses. 10 minutes.
- **Allowable expenses to log:** laptop and software, AI tools, hosting you pay on clients' behalf, domain, insurance, phone share, home office flat rate, travel to client sites.
- **Time:** 30 minutes to set up.
- **Cost:** £0 (spreadsheet or Mettle+FreeAgent) or about £10 to £15 a month for FreeAgent, Xero or QuickBooks standalone.
- **Link:** https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed

## 4. Invoice numbering

- **What:** Sequential numbers, never reused, never skipped. Format: `OW-0001`, `OW-0002`. Pegasus deposit and balance are OW-0001 and OW-0002.
- **Why:** HMRC expects a clear audit trail. Sequential numbers also make the spreadsheet trivial.
- **Each invoice must show:** the word Invoice, number, date, your name and trading name and address, client name and address, what it was for, amount, payment terms, bank details. No VAT number until registered.
- **How:** kit/INVOICE.html already has the layout. Add the number to the filename too.
- **Time:** None.
- **Cost:** £0.
- **Link:** https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers/invoices-what-they-must-include

## 5. Set aside 25 to 30 percent for tax

- **What:** Every time a payment lands, move 30 percent to a separate pot or savings account. Do not touch it.
- **Why:** 2026/27 rates: personal allowance £12,570, 20 percent income tax up to £50,270, 40 percent above. Class 4 National Insurance is 6 percent on profits from £12,570 to £50,270 and 2 percent above. Class 2 is no longer compulsory.
- **The maths at £100k profit:** income tax about £27,400, Class 4 NI about £3,250, total about £30,700. That is 31 percent. At £60k profit it is about £17,000, or 28 percent. So 30 percent is right for the target pace, and 25 percent is enough only in the first slow months.
- **Year two warning:** payments on account mean the January 2028 bill is the 2026/27 tax plus half of 2027/28 on top. Keep saving the 30 percent through 2027 and it covers it.
- **Making Tax Digital:** from April 2026 sole traders with over £50k turnover must keep digital records and send quarterly updates through software. The threshold drops to £30k in April 2027. On the 100k pace you will be in scope from April 2027 or April 2028 depending on the 2026/27 figure, so pick a tool in item 3 that is MTD-ready (FreeAgent, Xero, QuickBooks all are).
- **Time:** 2 minutes per payment.
- **Cost:** £0.
- **Links:** https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates , https://www.gov.uk/guidance/use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax
- **Sources:** [Bytestart sole trader tax calculator 2026/27](https://www.bytestart.co.uk/calculators/sole-trader-tax-calculator/), [Bytestart on MTD for sole traders](https://www.bytestart.co.uk/self-employed-accounts/mtd-sole-traders/), [HMRC press release on MTD](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/hm-revenue-customs-hmrc/pressreleases/436000-sole-traders-and-landlords-make-their-tax-digital-3460581)

## 6. VAT: when it bites

- **What:** You must register for VAT once taxable turnover in any rolling 12 months goes over £90,000, or if you expect to go over it in the next 30 days alone. Register within 30 days of crossing it.
- **Why it matters here:** The 100k pace crosses the threshold. At that point every invoice gets 20 percent added. Small business clients who are not VAT registered (many of yours) feel that as a price rise from £2,000 to £2,400. VAT registered clients do not care.
- **Options when you get close:** (a) register and put VAT on top, (b) register and absorb it (£2,000 inc VAT is £1,667 to you), (c) hold turnover under £90k with care weeks. Decide when the rolling 12 months hits about £75k. Check the rolling total monthly in MONEY.md.
- **Time:** 30 minutes to register online, then a quarterly return.
- **Cost:** £0 to register. Quarterly returns are easy in any MTD bookkeeping tool.
- **Link:** https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat
- **Sources:** [Avask on the 2026 threshold](https://avask.com/blog/uk-vat-threshold-2026/), [Simply Business VAT registration guide](https://www.simplybusiness.co.uk/knowledge/articles/2010/07/2010-07-06-VAT-registration---is-it-right-for-my-business/)

## 7. Professional indemnity insurance

- **What:** Covers you if a client claims your work caused them a loss (a booking app that lost bookings, a data mistake). Usually bundled with public liability.
- **Worth it?** Yes, from the third or fourth client. The terms cap liability at the fee, but a cap does not stop someone suing, and a few clients (bigger firms, anyone public-sector adjacent) will ask for proof of cover before signing. At this price it is cheaper than one hour arguing.
- **How much cover:** £100,000 to £250,000 is plenty for £2,000 builds. Add cyber cover if a client's customer data is involved, which it will be.
- **Cost:** Simply Business quotes developer PI from about £8 a month (around £92 to £120 a year) for £100k to £1m cover. NimbleFins average is about £130 a year for £100k cover and £470 for £1m.
- **Time:** 20 minutes to get a quote online.
- **Sources:** [Simply Business software developer insurance](https://www.simplybusiness.co.uk/business-insurance/software-developers-insurance/), [NimbleFins PI costs](https://www.nimblefins.co.uk/node/949)

## 8. ICO data protection fee

- **What:** Organisations that process personal data as a controller pay an annual fee to the Information Commissioner's Office.
- **Needed?** Almost certainly yes. You hold client contact details, and client apps hold their customers' or staff's data (the Pegasus rota holds staff names and shifts). When you host it under the care plan you are processing it. The ICO has said it is actively chasing unregistered controllers. Use the ICO self-assessment to confirm, then pay.
- **Cost:** Tier 1 (turnover under £632k, 10 or fewer staff): £52 a year, £47 by direct debit.
- **Time:** 15 minutes.
- **Link:** https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/
- **Sources:** [Lancashire Business View on the Feb 2025 fee rise](https://www.lancashirebusinessview.co.uk/latest-news-and-features/information-commissioner-increases-fees-7004), [Irwin Mitchell on ICO enforcement](https://www.irwinmitchell.com/news-and-insights/expert-comment/post/102fq0f/ico-stepping-up-plans-to-fine-data-controllers-whove-not-registered-or-paid-the)

## 9. Domain and business email

- **What:** Buy a domain (oneweek.something) and put the site on it instead of github.io. Set up one business mailbox, hello@ or alex@, and use it on the site, quote, invoice and terms. The site currently has a placeholder contact for this reason.
- **Why:** A gmail address and a github.io URL cost trust with a £2,000 decision. Email on your own domain also survives a change of provider.
- **How:** Domain from Cloudflare Registrar (at cost, no markup) or Porkbun. Email: Google Workspace Business Starter at £5.90 a user a month plus VAT on an annual plan, or Fastmail, or Zoho Mail which has a free tier for one domain. Point GitHub Pages at the domain (custom domain setting, add the CNAME record) so nothing else changes.
- **Time:** 1 hour including DNS.
- **Cost:** Domain about £10 to £15 a year for a .co.uk or .com, more for a short .uk or novelty TLD. Email £0 to £85 a year.
- **Links:** https://www.cloudflare.com/products/registrar/ , https://docs.github.com/en/pages/configuring-a-custom-domain-for-your-github-pages-site
- **Sources:** [G-Cloud Workspace pricing document, Jan 2026](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/720213/425755769029408-pricing-document-2026-01-29-2253.pdf)

## Not needed yet

- **Limited company.** Not worth it under about £50k profit; the admin and accountant cost eat the tax saving. Revisit when the care plan list passes 15 or if a client insists on contracting with a company.
- **Accountant.** Optional in year one. A one-off hour (£100 to £200) before the first tax return is good value. Worth having on retainer once VAT or a company is in play.
- **Trade mark.** Oneweek is a common word. Do not spend money defending it.
