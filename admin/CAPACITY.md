# Capacity: the weekly rhythm

One person plus AI agents. One build in flight at a time. The week is the unit.

## The week

| Day | What | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Kick-off call (30 min). Lock the one-page spec, confirm logins and content have arrived, set up repo, hosting and accounts. Agents scaffold the project while the call is still fresh. Spec signed off by lunch. Build starts in the afternoon. | 6 |
| **Tue to Thu** | Build. Agents write first drafts of screens, data layer and tests; Alex reviews, wires it to the real data, and fixes what the agents got wrong. End of each day: a short message to the client with a link to the live version. Thursday evening: everything that is going in is in. | 8 each |
| **Friday** | Morning: polish, test on the client's own devices, write the handover doc. Afternoon: handover call (45 min), walk through, hand over logins, send the final invoice. Done by 4pm. | 6 |

Friday after 4pm and the weekend are not for client work.

## Outreach

Outreach happens in fixed slots, not when the pipeline looks empty.

- **Monday 8 to 9am:** send 5 outreach messages (template in kit/OUTREACH.md) before the kick-off call.
- **Friday 4 to 5pm:** follow up anyone who replied in the week, send the quote to anyone who had a call, post the week's build as a short case note.
- **Calls** go in 30 minute slots at 8:30am or 5pm Tue to Thu so they do not cut into build time. Two calls a week is plenty.

Rule of thumb: 5 messages a week, 1 to 2 calls, about 1 booking every 2 weeks to start. Raise the message count, not the hours, if bookings are short.

## How many clients in flight

| State | Max at once | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Building | 1 | Never two. A split week is two half-built things. |
| Booked (deposit paid, week in the diary) | 6 | Six weeks of runway is enough. Past that, tell people the next date honestly and offer the waitlist. |
| Quoted, waiting for a yes | 4 | Chase once after 3 days, once after 7, then let it go. |
| Care plan | 15 | At roughly 4 hours each a month that is 60 hours, or 1.5 weeks a month. Beyond 10, block every fourth week as a care week with no build. |

A care week (no build, just care-plan work and admin) also makes a good buffer. Plan one every 6 to 8 weeks from the start, and more as the care list grows.

## When two people want the same week

The deposit holds the week. Nothing else does.

Say: "The week of the 9th is open until someone pays the deposit. If you would like it, pay the first £1,000 and it is yours. If someone else books it first I will offer you the next open week, which is the 16th."

If both pay on the same day, first payment in wins; the other gets the next week and a message the same day explaining why. Keep a short waitlist in MONEY.md: name, preferred week, next open week offered. If a week frees up (client moves or cancels), offer it to the waitlist in order, 24 hours each to say yes.

## What a bad week looks like, and the fix

- **Logins or content late.** The week still ends Friday. Build with placeholders, note it in the handover, and the missing bits go in a care-plan hour or a second week.
- **Scope creep on Wednesday.** Write it on the extras list, say "that is a second week", carry on.
- **Build is behind on Thursday night.** Cut features, not quality. Ship the core thing Friday, list the rest as a free 14-day fix if it was in the spec, or a second week if it was not.
- **Alex is ill.** Message the client Monday morning, offer to move the week or refund the deposit. Do not build sick.
