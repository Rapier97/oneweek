# Oneweek terms

Plain-English terms for a build week. Send with the quote. Not legal advice. If a client wants a longer contract, they can send theirs and we read it.

**Who:** Alex, trading as Oneweek, sole trader. "You" is the client named on the quote.

## The build week

1. **What you get.** The thing described in the signed-off one-page spec, working and handed over by the Friday of the booked week. The spec is the whole scope. If it is not in the spec, it is not in the week.
2. **Extras.** Anything that comes up during the week that is not in the spec gets written down. It goes in a second week, quoted separately, rather than slowing this one.
3. **Price.** £2,000 fixed. £1,000 to book the week, £1,000 on handover. No VAT is charged (Oneweek is not VAT registered). Invoices are due within 7 days.
4. **Booking.** The week is held once the first £1,000 is paid. Until then it is open to anyone.
5. **Moving the week.** You can move the week once with 7 days notice. Less than that, or a second move, and the deposit covers the lost week and a new deposit books a new one.
6. **What you provide.** Before the Monday: logins for domain, hosting, app stores and any system we connect to; the content (text, logo, photos, the spreadsheet it replaces); and one named person who can answer quick questions the same day. If these arrive late, the week still ends on the Friday.
7. **Ownership.** Once the final £1,000 is paid, the code, the accounts and everything built in the week are yours. Until then they are Alex's. Open-source libraries stay under their own licences.
8. **Handover.** You get a short written handover, the logins, and a walkthrough. After handover, fixes to things that do not match the spec are free for 14 days. After that, fixes are under the care plan or a new quote.
9. **App stores and third parties.** Alex submits to Apple, Google or any other third party on your behalf but cannot promise when or whether they approve. Their fees and their rules are yours.
10. **Liability.** Alex builds carefully but cannot be responsible for lost income or data caused by the software, by hosting providers or by third parties. Liability is capped at the fee you paid for the build.

## The care plan (optional)

11. **What it covers.** £200 a month covers hosting costs up to a reasonable size, fixes, security updates and small changes, up to roughly 4 hours a month. Hours do not roll over. Bigger changes are quoted as a second week.
12. **Starting and stopping.** Starts the month after handover if you want it. Either side can cancel with 30 days notice, in writing. If you cancel, you keep everything; Alex hands over hosting and walks you through it.
13. **Response.** Something broken: same working day. Small change: within the week.

## Your data

14. Alex only handles your data (and your customers' data) as needed to build and support the thing. It stays in the UK or the EU where the hosting allows. Copies on Alex's machines are deleted after handover, unless you are on the care plan, in which case they are kept only as long as needed to support you and deleted when the plan ends. Alex does not sell or share it with anyone else. If you need a formal data processing agreement, say so and we will sign one.

## The boring bit

15. These terms and the quote are the whole agreement. English law applies. If something goes wrong we talk first.

Agreed by paying the first invoice.
