# Money

## The 100k pace

Target: a £100k a year run rate by 31 Dec 2026, held through 2027.

| Mix | Builds a year | Care plan clients | Build income | Care income | Total |

Running total: build 1,650 / care 0 / paid 1,650
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Builds only | 50 | 0 | £100,000 | £0 | £100,000 |
| Steady | 40 | 10 | £80,000 | £24,000 | £104,000 |
| Comfortable | 35 | 15 | £70,000 | £36,000 | £106,000 |
| Half time | 26 | 25 | £52,000 | £60,000 | £112,000 |

- A build is £2,000. A care plan client is £200 a month, £2,400 a year.
- 50 builds a year means 50 of 52 weeks sold. Not realistic for long. Aim for the "steady" row: 40 builds (a booked week 3 out of 4) plus 10 care plans.
- Every care plan client takes about 4 hours a month, so 10 clients is a week a month. That is why 40 builds, not 50.
- Care plan is the thing that compounds. If 1 in 3 build clients takes it, 40 builds gives 13 care clients by the end of the year and about £31k a year of recurring income going into the next.
- Tax: keep 25 to 30 percent of everything that comes in (see SETUP_CHECKLIST.md). On £100k the take-home is roughly £70k to £73k.
- Monthly check: are there 3 to 4 builds in the month and is the care list growing? If yes, on pace.
